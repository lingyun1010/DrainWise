# Pitch outline (3 minutes)

Decided in ticket 05. No accuracy numbers anywhere: not on slides, not in the demo, not in Q&A.

## The one sentence

"DrainWise tells Parramatta's crews which gross pollutant traps (GPTs) to clean before they fill, suggests the truck run for the contractor, and turns every job's photos into proof of work."

## Story

Action. The council already has flood warning (FloodSmart / FLASH). We answer: which GPTs does the truck clean today, and where is the proof. The data flywheel is one line: "designed to become training data, nothing retrained yet."

## Challenge fit

Say "Challenge 5, AI for Smarter Asset and Environmental Management, with a nod to Challenge 1, Climate Resilience" at the start and at the close only. Submit under "City challenges", primary 5, secondary 1. Never two equal claims on one slide.

## Data-honesty lines

Say these out loud, once each.

- **Opening of step 2:** "Everything you'll see is sample data in real Parramatta locations. The risk model is trained on simulated history. We're asking the council for real records."
- **Close:** "This is sample data and a model trained on simulated history. With council records we swap the training data and retrain. The photos are designed to become training data; nothing is retrained yet."
- Keep the SAMPLE DATA label visible on every screen.
- Never call the demo formula an AI model.

- **Closing:** the next step (council asset locations and history), then the challenge name once more.
