# 05 Pitch and demo script

Type: grilling
Status: resolved (2026-11-08)
Blocked by: none

## Question

What do the judges see in 3 minutes, and which story carries it? Decide: the one-sentence pitch, the demo path through the product (manager ranked queue, truck run, crew proof, compliance records), what is shown live vs on a slide, how the data honesty is said out loud (sample data, simulated training, "designed to become training data"), and how we name the challenge fit (5 primary, 1 secondary). Depth vs breadth stays the standing default unless the 9 or 13 Nov answers change it.

## Decision (2026-11-08)

- **Story**: Action. Council already has flood warning (FloodSmart/FLASH); we answer "which drains does the truck clean today, and where is the proof". Flywheel is one line ("designed to become training data, nothing retrained yet"). No claims about prediction accuracy.
- **Pitch sentence**: "DrainWise tells Parramatta's crews which drains to clean before they block, plans the truck run, and turns every job's photos into proof of work."
- **3-minute path**:
  1. 0:00-0:25 problem and pitch (slide)
  2. 0:25-1:05 manager ranked queue; open a High asset and show its reasons
  3. 1:05-1:45 capacity slider to 2 m3, generate run, numbered stops and route
  4. 1:45-2:30 crew phone: one photo stop (BEFORE, AFTER, submit), one camera stop (Mark as cleaned)
  5. 2:30-2:50 compliance records show the job (before = training data, after = evidence)
  6. 2:50-3:00 close: data honesty and next step
- **Not in the main path**: 3D view, what-if sliders, rain forecast (Q&A only).
- **Data honesty**: said once at the start of step 2 ("Everything you'll see is sample data in real Parramatta locations. The risk model is trained on simulated history. We're asking the council for real records."), SAMPLE DATA label always visible, repeated once at the close. No accuracy numbers.
- **Challenge fit**: say "Challenge 5, AI for Smarter Asset and Environmental Management, with a nod to Challenge 1, Climate Resilience" at the start and the close only. Submit under "City challenges", primary 5, secondary 1. Never two equal claims on a slide.
- **Live vs slide**: steps 2-5 live (crew via side-by-side or a second device), Reset sample session before each run. Problem, pitch, close and data reality on slides.
- **Fallback**: pre-record a 60-90 s screen recording of steps 2-5 on the same sample data; switch to it if live stalls for more than 10 s. Recording is not committed to git.
- Depth vs breadth stays the default unless the 9 or 13 Nov answers change it.
