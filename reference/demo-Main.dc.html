FloodReady

Clean the right drains before they block.

Leaves block drain inlets and gross pollutant traps, and most of them have no camera. FloodReady predicts which ones are about to block and tells crews where to go first. Crews fill the truck on one run, and every job is closed with proof: a camera check or before and after photos. Pick a role to start. No sign-in needed.

Sample data only. Real deployment would use council single sign-on and audit logs.
